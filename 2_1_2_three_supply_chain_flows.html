Key takeaway

One network, three connected flows

The flows happen at the same time, often move in different directions, and depend on one another.

✓  Task complete
Physical flow

Materials and products move through factories, warehouses, transport links and customer locations.

Information flow

Orders and data coordinate what should be produced, stored, shipped and delivered.

Financial flow

Payments and invoices compensate the participants that supply the product.

Connected, not separate: the parcel cannot reach the customer without the right information, and the network cannot keep operating without financial flows. Physical flow is also commonly called material flow.
