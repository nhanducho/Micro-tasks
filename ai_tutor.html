Key takeaway

Interpretations depend on perspective and capability.

The supplier bankruptcy creates a shared external shock, but its consequences are not automatically identical for every company or department.

The same event can lead to different interpretations.

Some people and departments may emphasize supply shortages, operational disruption, higher costs, or customer losses.

Others may see a chance to secure scarce capacity, attract the competitor’s customers, redesign the product, or strengthen the company’s position.

Effective supply chain management brings these perspectives together. The value of an opportunity depends on whether the company has the capabilities to act on it while controlling the associated risks.
