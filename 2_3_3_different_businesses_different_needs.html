Three businesses, three different operating needs

Each business balances speed, customization, and risk differently.

Key takeaway

There is no universally correct decoupling point.

The best position depends on the product, the customer promise, and the risk of preparing inventory before demand is known.

Standard drill · Downstream · MTS

Fast delivery, low variety, and finished-goods inventory based on forecasts.

Configurable laptop · Middle · ATO

Standard modules provide speed, while final configuration provides variety.

Fitted kitchen · Upstream · MTO

Customer specifications guide production, reducing wrong-product risk but increasing lead time.

Speed favours downstream. Customization favours upstream. Modularity makes the middle possible.
