Key takeaway

The customer sees one result. The supply chain creates it together.

A late delivery is rarely an isolated event. It often begins with a missing information flow, an uncoordinated decision, or a poorly timed handover.

Internal information

Sales needs current inventory information before promising a delivery date.

Internal action

Warehouse stock signals must reach Purchasing early enough to trigger replenishment.

External coordination

The warehouse and logistics provider must align parcel readiness, pickup, and delivery.

Practical lesson: Supply-chain performance improves when internal departments and external partners share timely information and coordinate their actions around the same customer promise.
