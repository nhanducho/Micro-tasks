Flow interruption challenge

What happens when one flow stops?

The drill supply chain is operating normally. Components, information, and money move between participants—until one flow suddenly fails.

Information flow

Orders, schedules, stock records, shipping instructions, and confirmations coordinate action.

×
Without it, participants do not know what to do.
Physical flow

Components and completed drills move through facilities and transport links.

×
Without it, production and delivery cannot continue.
Financial flow

Customer payments, invoices, freight charges, and supplier payments sustain participation.

×
Without it, future supply and cooperation are threatened.

Key takeaway

The three flows are interdependent.

Different starting points. The same network-wide consequence.

Information stops first

Participants do not know what to order, produce, release, or deliver. Physical movement and payment are delayed.

Physical flow stops first

The required component or product does not arrive. Production, inventory availability, and delivery stop.

Financial flow stops first

A participant is not compensated. Future supply, capacity, and cooperation may soon stop.
